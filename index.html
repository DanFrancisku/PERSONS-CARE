About us

At Persons Care, we see the person first. Every person we support is unique, with their own strengths, interests and ways of communicating.

Our trained team works with each person and their family to build a life that feels safe, happy and meaningful – at a pace that is right for them.

Our care

We follow the needs of each person. Our support is planned with them, their family and other professionals.

💬

Communication

We use each person's way of talking – words, pictures, signs, or routines – so they feel heard.

🌿

Calm spaces

Quiet, low-stimulation rooms help people feel safe and manage sensory needs.

🤝

Positive behaviour support

We understand what each person needs and respond with kindness, not punishment.

📋

Person-centred plans

Every person has their own care plan, reviewed often with them and their family.

🎨

Life skills & activities

Cooking, shopping, hobbies, trips and learning – building confidence every day.

❤️

Health & wellbeing

We support medication, appointments and healthy living, working with local health teams.
